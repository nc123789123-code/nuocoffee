FAQ

The details.

Is this 1:1 networking or groups?

Groups — small ones, 4–6 people. Same vertical, similar level. A group is less awkward than a cold 1:1, and you walk away having met several peers, not one.

How do you keep the quality high?

We seat you by vertical and role, and everyone tells us what they're hoping to get out of it — so the table is people with real intent, not tourists. Share your LinkedIn if you'd like; no work email required.

Why start with NYC finance?

It's dense, tight-knit, and networking-driven — the ideal place to prove that peer-matched group coffee actually works. Other cities and industries follow.

What does it cost?

Free while we're brewing. Longer term we expect a light membership — priced to filter for people who actually show up, not to gate the community.

My vertical isn't listed.

Pick the closest one and tell us in the form. New verticals open as soon as there's enough demand to fill real tables.
