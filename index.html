FAQ

The details.

Is this 1:1 networking or groups?

Groups — small ones, 4–6 people. Same vertical, similar level. Group settings are less awkward than a cold 1:1 and you walk away having met several peers, not one.

How do you keep the quality high?

Work-email verification, and we match by vertical and seniority so you're with genuine peers. No recruiters or vendors cold-pitching the room.

Why start with NYC finance?

It's dense, tight-knit, and networking-driven — the ideal place to prove that peer-matched group coffee actually works. Other cities and industries follow.

What does it cost?

Free during early access. Longer term we expect a light membership — priced to filter for people who actually show up, not to gate the community.
