Perps in 30 seconds

Like futures, but no expiry. Funding payments between longs and shorts keep the price near spot. Example: long $100k of BTC exposure with $10k margin is 10× leverage; a small move against you can trigger liquidation, depending on maintenance margin.

  1. Will Nasdaq and CME eventually need to trade 24/7?Follow-up: on a weekend shock, is on-chain price discovery real, or thin liquidity? Who would you trust for the Monday open?
  2. Will stablecoins challenge banks' payment and deposit economics, or become infrastructure banks use themselves?Hook: the OCC's GENIUS Act rule takes effect Jan 18. Do banks issue their own?
  3. Does tokenization create new liquidity, or just change the plumbing?Make the distinction: a perp on a stock ≠ a tokenized share with shareholder rights.
Your angleA clearinghouse is a credit product: margin, default waterfall, mutualized losses. On-chain venues replace the clearinghouse with code, oracles and a liquidation engine. Same credit questions, new plumbing: who absorbs losses when liquidations can't keep up?

Transition: “The venue that's pulled the most of this into one place is Hyperliquid, so let's dig in.”

2 · Hyperliquid deep dive 4:50–5:15

Goal: from “people trade crypto there” to “exchange infrastructure is becoming programmable”.

The four concepts

  • Perps on a central limit order book (like Nasdaq), not an AMM pool like Uniswap.
  • HyperCore = the trading engine; HyperEVM = Ethereum-compatible apps built around it.
  • HIP-3: outside builders can deploy their own perp markets, subject to staking HYPE and other requirements. Product creation is no longer only the exchange's call.
  • HIP-4: outcome (prediction) markets on the same engine; mainnet since May 2026, as reported.

Numbers (say “about”)

  • Hyperliquid, this week: about $10.7B of perp volume in 24 hours (Nov 19) and about $5.5B of open interest (Nov 20). Trackers differ by a few billion depending on how they count builder (HIP-3) markets. Source: DefiLlama · The Block data
  • Q3 peak: open interest reached $16.76B at the end of September on a two-sided basis, per the Hyperliquid Research Collective's Q3 report, so today's level is well below the peak. Source: HRC Q3 report (via BigGo)
  • Nov 19, Washington: CFTC Chairman Michael Selig said on-chain venues like Hyperliquid can serve U.S. customers by registering as an exchange or partnering with a registered one, with the same rules as everyone else. It was not an approval, and Hyperliquid has not announced an application. Source: The Block · Crypto Briefing
  • Stablecoins: the OCC finalized its GENIUS Act rule for payment stablecoin issuers on Oct 21; it takes effect Jan 18, 2027. Other regulators' rules are still in progress. Source: OCC · Skadden
  • Prediction markets on Hyperliquid: HIP-4 outcome markets went from testnet in February to mainnet in May 2026, starting with a daily BTC binary market (as reported). Source: CCN · CoinGecko
  1. Why does Hyperliquid need its own blockchain?Answer if asked: trading needs high throughput and predictable execution; a purpose-built chain can optimize for that. The trade-off is decentralization and validator concentration.
  2. Why has it attracted so much volume?Let the room answer: fees, speed, no KYC, listings, incentives, the token. Then: which of those survive regulation?
  3. Is Hyperliquid becoming the infrastructure for a new derivatives ecosystem, rather than just another venue?
  4. What's the biggest risk of a decentralized derivatives exchange?Oracle manipulation, stressed liquidity, forced liquidations, infrastructure failures, validator or governance concentration. Transparency doesn't remove market risk.

Glossary

Perp
Perpetual future: a futures-like contract with no expiry. Periodic funding payments between longs and shorts keep its price near spot.
Funding rate
If the perp trades above spot, longs pay shorts (and vice versa).
Open interest
The total size of positions still open.
CEX / DEX
Centralized exchange (e.g. Coinbase) vs. decentralized exchange running on a blockchain.
AMM
Automated market maker: a liquidity pool prices trades with a formula (e.g. Uniswap). No order book.
CLOB
Central limit order book: bids and offers matched by price and time, like Nasdaq. Hyperliquid's model.
HyperCore
Hyperliquid's trading and execution engine.
HyperEVM
Its Ethereum-compatible smart-contract layer for apps built around HyperCore.
HIP-3
Lets outside builders deploy their own perp markets, subject to staking HYPE and other requirements.
HIP-4
Outcome (prediction) markets on Hyperliquid.
HLP
Hyperliquidity Provider: a community vault that makes markets and takes part in liquidations.
Oracle
The price feed a protocol trusts for marks and liquidations; a common point of attack.
Stablecoin
A token meant to hold $1, backed by reserves (e.g. USDC, USDT).
RWA
Real-world assets (treasuries, stocks, credit) represented on a blockchain.
