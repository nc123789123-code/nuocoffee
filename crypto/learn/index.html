Part 1

Blockchain & smart contracts 区块链与智能合约

Blockchain 区块链

A blockchain is a shared digital ledger kept by a network of computers rather than one institution. It records transactions according to agreed rules.可以理解为一本由多方共同维护的电子账本。在银行，你的余额由银行内部系统记录；在区块链上，余额和转账由网络共同验证和记录，已确认的交易很难被单方修改。
Bank 银行One institution keeps the ledger

一家机构维护账本

Blockchain 区块链Many nodes verify the ledger

多个节点共同验证

Why it matters: different parties can share one transaction system without trusting a single company to keep the records. But it does not guarantee a transaction is legal, an asset is real, or the code has no bugs. 不同区块链的去中心化程度和安全性也不一样。

Smart contract 智能合约

A program deployed on a blockchain that executes pre-set rules automatically.部署在区块链上的程序，条件满足时自动执行。

Private credit example 例子：a borrower pays $1M of interest in a supported digital asset; the contract automatically sends $600k to Investor A and $400k to Investor B. TradFi can automate this too; the difference is that every party can verify the result on one shared system, and other apps can plug into it. 这就是 programmability（可编程性）。

Part 2

Binance vs. Hyperliquid vs. Uniswap

这是最需要理解的区别。

CEX · 中心化交易所Binance

你把资产存进 Binance，它负责撮合、记账和提现。

Key idea: trust the operator.

10 月被盗约 63 亿美元，用户靠 Binance 自有资金补足：用户的安全取决于一家公司。

On-chain order book DEXHyperliquid

用自己控制的钱包下单，订单、成交和持仓由它自己的区块链处理。

Key idea: trade through blockchain infrastructure.

AMM · 自动做市Uniswap

不用订单簿，而是和流动性池兑换，价格由智能合约按池内资产计算。

Key idea: trade against a pool.

买 $10,000 ETH，三种方式 · Buying $10k of ETH

  • Binance: 在 Binance 下单，内部撮合系统匹配买卖双方并更新你的账户余额。
  • Hyperliquid: 用钱包控制的账户下单，链上交易系统撮合并更新状态。
  • Uniswap: 用 USDC 和池子换 ETH，合约按池内流动性和定价规则完成兑换。

买卖双方挂出愿意成交的价格，系统撮合。Liquidity pool 流动性池则像一个自动兑换池：池里有 ETH 和 USDC，你按合约定价直接兑换，不用等别人挂单。

You can say

Binance and Hyperliquid both use order books, but Binance runs its matching engine centrally, while Hyperliquid processes trading on its blockchain. Uniswap uses a different model based on liquidity pools.

Part 3

Perpetual futures 永续合约

A derivative that tracks an underlying asset's price with no fixed expiry. You can go long or short, with leverage.没有固定到期日的衍生品，可以做多、做空、加杠杆。是 Hyperliquid 的核心产品。
Leverage calculator 杠杆模拟
Position 名义本金
P&L 浮动盈亏
Return on margin 保证金回报

Simplified long: ignores funding, fees and slippage. Liquidation happens earlier than shown because of maintenance margin. 简化模型，未计资金费率、手续费和滑点；实际会因维持保证金提前强平。

Funding rate 资金费率

  • Perp above spot → longs usually pay shorts. 合约价高于现货：通常多头付空头。
  • Perp below spot → shorts usually pay longs. 合约价低于现货：通常空头付多头。
  • It's a payment between traders, not usually an exchange fee. 是多空之间的支付，通常不是交易所的收入。

Why have perpetual futures become so popular in crypto, compared with dated futures?
参考：不用定期展期，适合持续持有杠杆仓位；但资金费率可能很高，杠杆清算风险也大。

Part 4

Stablecoins 稳定币

A token designed to hold a stable price, usually $1. USDC is issued by Circle, USDT by Tether.旨在维持稳定价值的数字代币，通常与美元 1:1 挂钩。$1,000 可以换成 1,000 USDC，在支持的链上转账、交易、支付或用于 DeFi。发行商持有现金和短期国债等储备，支持符合条件的赎回。
Not a bank deposit: USDC is not FDIC-insured. Holders take issuer, reserve, custody and de-peg risk. 不是有 FDIC 保险的存款。
Regulation: the OCC's GENIUS Act rule for payment stablecoin issuers was finalized Oct 21 and takes effect Jan 18, 2027. Source: OCC

Why banks care 银行为什么关心：跨境支付要经过多家中介，受结算时间和时区限制；稳定币可以全天候转移数字美元。但最终换回银行账户里的美元，仍然需要金融机构和合规安排。

You can say

Stablecoins bring a familiar asset, the U.S. dollar, onto blockchain rails. The value isn't a new currency; it's faster, more programmable transfers of dollars.

Part 5 · your strongest topic

RWA & tokenization 资产代币化

Tokenization represents the rights or economic interest in an asset as tokens on a blockchain.把某项资产的权利或经济利益表示为链上代币。例如一个基金持有 1 亿美元的 Private Credit 资产：
Borrower 借款人$100M loan
loan cash flows ↓
Fund / SPVholds the loan, issues tokens
tokenized interests ↓
Investor A
Investor B
Investor C

Illustrative. Legal rights depend on the SPV, fund documents and token terms; a token isn't necessarily a direct claim on the borrower. 示意图，实际权利取决于 SPV、基金文件和代币条款。

Why tokenize a loan? 为什么上链

  • Faster settlement 减少人工结算步骤
  • Fractional ownership 更容易拆成小份额
  • Programmable distributions 自动分配利息、执行转让限制
  • Shared recordkeeping 在共享账本上记录持有人和交易
Tokenization does not eliminate credit risk. 借款人违约，代币照样亏损。没有买家，即使 24/7 可转让也没有真正的流动性。Credit angle: daily-tradable tokens on quarterly-marked loans create a liquidity and valuation mismatch.
Securitization 证券化Tokenization 代币化
主要目的资产池融资、风险分层数字化表示和转让权益
改变信用风险分配？可以（tranching）不一定
需要 SPV？经常需要视结构而定
需要区块链？不需要通常需要
创造流动性？取决于证券和市场同样不保证
You can say

Securitization changes how risk and cash flows are structured. Tokenization changes how ownership is recorded and transferred. The two can coexist, for example a tokenized tranche of a securitization.

Part 6

What is Hyperliquid trying to become?

它不只想做 BTC、ETH 永续合约。基础设施包括：

  • HyperCore: 交易和核心金融功能的执行层 · the trading engine.
  • HyperEVM: 支持开发者构建智能合约应用 · Ethereum-compatible apps.
  • HIP-3: 符合要求（质押 HYPE 等）的开发者可以部署新的永续合约市场，不用从零搭交易所。
  • HIP-4: 预测 / 结果市场，据报道 2026 年 5 月上线主网。

它仍然依赖预言机（价格来源）、流动性、风险管理和监管框架。本周规模：24 小时永续成交约 $10.7B，未平仓约 $5.5B。Source: DefiLlama

An important nuance

Binance has APIs and its own BNB Chain ecosystem, so it isn't "not programmable". The difference is that Hyperliquid's trading engine and its on-chain app ecosystem are directly connected. 区别不是 Binance 不可编程，而是 Hyperliquid 的交易基础设施和链上应用生态连接得更直接。
